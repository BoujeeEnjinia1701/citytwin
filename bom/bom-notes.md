# BOM notes

Costs are indicative estimates for a single prototype, in USD. Only the e-paper display price was checked against the maker's own listing (September 2026). Suppliers are not yet selected.

- **Kiosk parts** (items 1 to 12, 14 and 15): about $739.
- **TwinKit gateway** (item 13): about $285, from the TwinKit README. It is not part of the kiosk cost, because one gateway can serve a kiosk and a whole neighborhood of nodes, and a city that already runs TwinKit only needs the kiosk.
- **Kiosk with gateway**: about $1,024, above the $600 budget in `project.yaml`. See `docs/REVIEW.md` for the proposed options, awaiting Amish.
- Items 16 to 18 are software and documents with no parts cost. Hosting for the public open data files is not included.
- Not included: concrete footing, mains connection by an electrician, permits, and installation labor.
